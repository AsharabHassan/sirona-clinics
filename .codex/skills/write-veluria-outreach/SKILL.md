# VELURIA outreach

## Personalise first

- Research the clinic, recipient, current role, services, and one verifiable clinic signal before drafting.
- Write every message individually. Do not reuse identical sentences across recipients.
- Connect the clinic signal naturally to VELURIA without inventing familiarity, results, or prior contact.
- Use the recipient's verified first name and the clinic's correct trading name.

## Write clean copy

- Use natural British English.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes. Restructure the sentence or use commas, colons, or full stops.
- Check grammar, spelling, punctuation, capitalisation, spacing, names, links, dates, and times before approval and again immediately before sending.
- Prefer short, direct sentences and natural paragraphs.
- Avoid awkward jargon, exaggerated language, artificial enthusiasm, and generic template phrases.
- Keep hyphens only where grammatically appropriate, such as `enzyme-based` and `before-and-after`.
- Never include internal notes, placeholders, unsupported disclaimers, or AI-generated caveats in outreach copy.
- Check for a platform-generated signature before sending and do not duplicate the sign-off in the message body.

## Position the offer

- Introduce PBSerum VELURIA before promoting the patient acquisition system.
- Describe VELURIA accurately as a professional enzyme-based bioremodelling range for aesthetic clinics.
- In the first touch, explain the enzyme-based concept and invite the recipient to experience the personalised landing page.
- Introduce Sirona's patient education and acquisition support in a later nudge.
- Do not promise guaranteed patients, revenue, or clinical outcomes.

## Email hygiene

- Send future campaign emails from `hello@sironaaesthetics.co.uk`.
- Confirm the recipient, verified address, suppression status, DND state, sender identity, and link destination before sending.
- Include exactly one working HighLevel unsubscribe mechanism. Use `{{unsubscribe}}` when the account's default unsubscribe footer is not already enabled.
- Do not combine `{{unsubscribe}}` with an enabled default footer.
- Stop for a suppression conflict, hard bounce, authentication failure, or location mismatch.

## Pre-send gate

Do not send until all checks pass:

1. Confirm the person and clinic match.
2. Confirm every personalised statement against its source.
3. Read the message aloud for natural flow.
4. Remove em dashes and unnecessary disclaimers.
5. Run a final grammar and punctuation check.
6. Confirm the correct link, sender, recipient, and unsubscribe method.
7. Obtain approval when the campaign requires it.
